Use the buyer-questions skill to generate the list. This command only generates questions and measures nothing.

Take the inputs from these arguments: $ARGUMENTS

Pick out the business name, the city or area served and what the business does. Pick out any products (up to four), competitors (up to three) and one differentiator the arguments carry. Ask the user for whichever of the three required inputs is missing, then run the skill's script and show the grouped result.
